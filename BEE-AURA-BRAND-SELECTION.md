# Bee-Aura Brand Selection

## Status

APPROVED BRAND DIRECTION — Concept 17

## Decision

The selected Bee-Aura visual direction is Concept 17.

This direction should guide the app shell, sidebar, home page, dashboard, core components, and final page polish.

## Visual standard

Bee-Aura should feel:

- premium
- professional
- calm
- controlled
- trustworthy
- modern
- useful to real UK service businesses

## Theme direction

Use:

- dark navy / black premium SaaS base
- gold logo and primary accents
- blue highlights for active/system/technology states
- Bee-Aura wordmark
- descriptor: Lead Recovery OS or Service Business OS
- polished Aura Assistant visual
- professional dashboard/chart styling

## Avoid

Do not use:

- BA placeholder logo
- AI placeholder badge
- emoji bee
- cheap robot
- childish cartoon mascot
- screenshot crops as final assets
- generic AI robot styling
- broad layout redesigns without approval

## Implementation rule

Keep the page layouts already chosen.

Apply the Concept 17 theme and assets across the system in controlled passes, one section or page at a time.
