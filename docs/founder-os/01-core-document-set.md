# Bee-Aura Founder OS — Core Document Set v1.0

## Purpose

This document set exists to stop Bee-Aura becoming messy, unsafe, or dependent on memory. It gives the founder and ChatGPT one shared operating system for decisions, coding, sales, research, governance, customer pilots and future SaaS build work.

The goal is simple:

ChatGPT controls the workflow.  
The founder controls the gates.  
Evidence unlocks progress.

---

## Current stage

Bee-Aura is currently a polished fake-data demo moving toward a pilot-ready MVP.

Current position:

Lead Recovery OS for UK service businesses.

Current product story:

Bee-Aura helps service businesses recover opportunities their current tools, inboxes and workflows still let slip.

Current demo scope:

- Fake data only
- Client-safe wording
- No live customer data
- No live integrations
- No payments
- No production AI actions
- No full invoicing
- No full CRM replacement
- No full field-service replacement

## 06 — Decision Log

Purpose: Records final decisions so they do not need to be debated again.

Each entry should include:

- Date
- Decision
- Status: VERIFIED / DESIGN / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED
- Reason
- Evidence
- Risk
- Owner approval
- Follow-up action

Used by:

- Founder
- ChatGPT
- Future handovers

Update after every real decision.

## 13 — Sales Demo Script

Purpose: Makes the demo tell one clear story instead of becoming a page tour.

Core demo story:

Bee-Aura finds the leads, replies, bookings, follow-ups and reviews that are most at risk of being missed.

Demo journey:

1. Home — what Bee-Aura is
2. Dashboard — what needs attention now
3. Messages/Leads — missed opportunity recovery
4. Bookings — booking protection
5. Follow-Ups — stale lead recovery
6. Reviews — review recovery
7. Activity Log / Error Log — trust and control
