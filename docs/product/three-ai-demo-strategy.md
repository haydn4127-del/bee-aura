# Bee-Aura Three AI Demo Strategy

Status: DESIGN  
Branch: today-first-simplified-demo-v1

## Core decision

Bee-Aura will now have three separate demo experiences:

1. Solo Demo
2. Team Demo
3. Control Centre Demo

These are not three separate products. They are three demo experiences powered by the same Bee-Aura system, brand, product logic and AI assistant: Aura.

## Master rule

Bee-Aura should use as much AI as possible where it creates speed, clarity, recovery, proof or control.

AI may draft, summarise, prioritise, explain, prepare and recommend.

Founder / owner approval remains required for risky actions.

## Demo 1 — Solo

For one-person trades and owner-operators.

The Solo demo should feel:
- simple
- fast
- mobile-friendly
- no CRM feeling
- no admin overload
- focused on what needs doing now

Main story:
Open Bee-Aura, see Today, reply to urgent leads, check jobs, send follow-ups, request reviews.

AI behaviour:
- spots missed leads
- prioritises Today
- drafts replies
- suggests call-backs
- prepares follow-ups
- prepares review requests
- explains why an action matters

## Demo 2 — Team

For small service businesses with an admin person, receptionist, coordinator or multiple workers.

The Team demo should show:
- shared inbox
- assigned work
- owner approvals
- coordinator view
- team notes
- responsibility clarity

Main story:
The team can handle daily work, while the owner still controls risky decisions.

AI behaviour:
- suggests assignments
- summarises workload
- flags owner approval items
- prevents duplicate replies
- prepares handover notes
- shows who needs to act next

## Demo 3 — Control Centre

For premium users, bigger businesses, or the full desktop command centre.

The Control Centre demo should show:
- reporting
- proof pack
- business performance
- system history
- needs fixing
- reviews
- settings
- team permissions
- deeper owner/admin control

Main story:
The owner gets full visibility, proof and control over the business system.

AI behaviour:
- summarises performance
- finds weak spots
- shows money at risk
- watches errors
- prepares reports
- builds proof packs
- suggests operational improvements

## Mobile rule

Mobile always stays simple across all demos.

Mobile should show:
- Today
- Inbox
- Jobs
- Customers
- urgent actions
- approval cards

Mobile should not show dense desktop/admin pages in portrait.

## Safety rule

Fake data only until reviewed.

No live customer data.
No live WhatsApp/SMS.
No live payments.
No live bookings.
No live integrations.
No AI auto-sending real replies.

## Product promise rule

Bee-Aura marketing must be proof-backed.

Do not claim:
- guaranteed revenue
- exact hours saved
- every missed lead prevented
- every opportunity recovered

Use safer wording:
Bee-Aura is designed to help service businesses spot missed opportunities, respond faster, manage follow-ups and keep owner control visible.
